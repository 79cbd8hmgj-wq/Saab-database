# Saab candidate scope — model years 1998–2011

**Decision:** Research potential project cars among **all factory automatic, front-wheel-drive, non-convertible Saab 9-3 and 9-5 models** from model years **1998 to 2011 inclusive**, **originally offered in the United States or Canada**.

## Hard exclusions

- Manual transmissions (even if an automatic was offered elsewhere for that model year).
- Convertibles.
- All-wheel drive, including Saab XWD / 9-3X XWD; do not treat a FWD trim's catalogue as evidence of an AWD candidate's eligibility.
- Models other than Saab 9-3 and 9-5, and model years outside the range.

## Screening families (not confirmed production combinations)

| Model | Platform | Years to screen | Potential qualifying body styles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9-3 original | 9400 | 1998–2003 | 3/5-door hatchbacks when factory automatic/FWD offered |
| 9-3 second generation | 9440 | 2003–2011 | Sport Sedan, SportCombi/wagon when factory automatic/FWD offered |
| 9-5 original | 9600 | 1998–2009 | Sedan, SportCombi/wagon when factory automatic/FWD offered |
| 9-5 second generation | 650 | 2010–2011 | Production sedan when factory automatic/FWD offered |

Only original US- and Canadian-market factory configurations qualify; other-market-only models remain reference information and are not purchase candidates. Exact market, year, engine, trim, body, gearbox, and driveline combinations must be corroborated in original documentation. Year overlap (especially 2003 9-3) does not imply the generations are interchangeable. A 2011 model year or rare engine cannot be presumed available in either the US or Canada; verify each market separately. Do not add hypothetical 9-5 NG wagons as ordinary production candidates.

## Rules for the research program

1. Record every actual candidate configuration as **unassessed**, **partially researched**, or **evidence-backed**, independently of how much data the other generation has.
2. Preserve existing manual, convertible, and XWD reference material: it remains useful for preventing incorrect part swaps, but these are not project-car candidates.
3. Prioritize non-running engine/automatic transmission risks, chassis corrosion, brake and steering safety, parts supply and repair-cost exposure, with the user aiming to avoid engine/transmission rebuilds.
4. Do not equate supplier cross-references with approved swaps, source counts with reliability, or eligibility with a buying recommendation.
5. Existing Phase 0 gate status remains **A partial; B and C unmet**. This decision expands the candidate denominator, not the research completeness census.
