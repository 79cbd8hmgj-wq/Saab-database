# Phase 0 Gate A — structural corrosion and underbody safety inspection map

**Research date:** 2026-10-13. Vehicles: original Saab **9-5 (9600)**, including a **5-door/SportCombi wagon**, and second-generation **9-3 (9440)**, including **convertible (CV)**. Factory body repairs cited mainly come from **MY2006** WIS; US 9-5 fuel tank detail is **MY2008**, and local safety inspection criteria are published by **New York DMV**. This is **not** a completed inspection of any car, a corrosion frequency study, a certified DIY repair plan, or a model-year-free VIN part approval.

**Gate outcome:** A qualified **inspection plan** now has more exact mounting and component targets. **Gate A is still PARTIAL** because no actual candidate structure, installed mount condition, body geometry, year-specific repair changes or professional quote has been examined.

## Inspection priority and evidence tags

**Documented factory / legal criterion:** specifics directly shown in linked Saab WIS or NY DMV. **Project inspection inference:** prioritizing those areas on a 20-year-old Northeast project car because load-bearing, brake and fuel components are at risk. Saab does **not** say these locations are the most frequent corrosion failure points. **Unknown:** whether a particular vehicle's metal is sound, how much cross-section is lost, what can safely be welded, or whether a repair is economical.

### 1. Original 9-5 MY2006 factory structure, with wagon-specific zones

| Zone to photograph and have examined | Direct source evidence | What it can—and cannot—establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rear suspension subframe and its bushes**: left/right frame tubes, link arms, mounting bolts, bush sleeves and brackets | [MY2006 bush/support arm procedure](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/7-suspension-wheels/rear-suspension/adjustment-replacement/bush-support-arm): bush service can require lowering one side of subframe; factory specifies final rear toe adjustment | Bolt-on bushing replacement is distinct from repairing frame metal or the body mount. A photograph cannot confirm section thickness or safe threaded engagement. |
| **Body-mounted rear subframe captive nuts**: floor metal around retaining point, plug/access area from cabin, underside at bolt entry | [MY2006 rear nut-piece procedure](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/nut-piece-rear-subframe) describes floor-side **35 mm marked access hole**, new captive nut and anticorrosion-coated plug | A factory repair for a **nut piece** is NOT permission to reuse a bolt anchored in cracked/rust-thinned body material. Do not substitute patch plates or cut ahead of the defined repair seam. |
| **SportCombi 5D rear luggage floor, crossmember and reinforcements** | [MY2006 5D rear floor](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/section-of-rear-floor-5d): floor attaches to frame members, wheel housings and crossmember; welding, primer, seam sealer, underside PVC and cavity treatment required | The **50 mm floor cut placement** is a body repair instruction, NOT a factory 'safe 50 mm rust' threshold. Some MIG-brazed joints must be restored with MIG brazing—not conventional weld. |
| **5D rear wheelhouse, outer sill, C/D pillars and underlying reinforcement** | [MY2006 5D partial side panel](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/side-panel-5d-part-of-side-panel) warns against cutting inside C-pillar reinforcement; joining method depends on source illustration | Visible outer skin corrosion can hide a separate structural repair. Damage extent cannot be judged from a quick quarter-panel photo. |
| **Front engine/powertrain subframe and its body attachment points** | [MY2006 petrol front subframe](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/7-suspension-wheels/front-suspension/adjustment-replacement/subframe-petrol-engines) carries steering gear, suspension arms, engine/gearbox torque arm, pipe brackets and radiator support | Rust of the frame raises several questions; it does not by itself prove the steering/suspension is failed, nor guarantee mounts are sound. |
| **Fuel tank, filler area, rear exhaust/handbrake routing** | [MY2008 US fuel tank](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/fuel-system-petrol/adjustment-replacement/fuel-tank-us-incl-e85): feed and return, bleeder line, heat shield, tank straps and parking-brake cables are interacting underbody systems | Fuel hose refit may require new hoses. Never assume 'no puddle' establishes safe old hoses or lines. No drilling/grinding/welding near suspected leaks. |
| **Lifting support regions** | [MY2006 9-5 lift points](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/technical-data/lifting-and-attachment-points): factory uses front towing-eye/subframe area, sill jack brackets and rear tow point/towbar | Factory **forbids lifting under rear axle or spare-wheel well**. The listed lift point also needs to be sound: never place yourself underneath an inadequately supported or corroded shell. |

**Specific buying use case:** A claimed **2008 9-5 wagon with 'rear subframe rust'** is **not** categorized as a bargain 'just replace the subframe' repair. First document whether the rust is confined to removable frame/subframe/links/bushes, or extends into captive nuts, suspension pickup body metal, rear floor, longitudinal frame members or adjacent brake/fuel pipes. The latter path needs a body shop with Saab-approved joining capability, alignment measurement, and an estimate. Our best detailed **MY2006** body repair sources do **not** themselves establish every **MY2008** part or structural revision.

### 3. New York actual vehicle inspection requirements (state regulator, not Saab)

The [New York DMV safety/emissions inspection guide](https://dmv.ny.gov/new-york-state-vehicle-safetyemissions-inspection-program) currently says certified inspectors check:
- **Chassis/frame:** breaks, cracks or **severe rust at suspension attachment points**.
- **Brake lines/hoses:** leaks, cracks, chafing, restrictions and improper support, plus brake system performance.
- **Fuel:** **any leak causing dripping or pooling is unacceptable**.
- **Inspection administration:** generally annual (12 months), inspection by a certified inspector at licensed facility. DIY or other-shop repair may be performed, but a new passing inspection is needed for an inspection sticker.

These are state **minimum safety criteria** for covered NY-registered cars, not a factory numeric pass/fail thickness for a Saab unibody. The complete regulated inspector decision is not something this research database can issue. An old inspection sticker or seller claim of a past pass does not certify a car today.

## Prepurchase collection sheet — request this evidence, do not invent it

For the seller / photographer:
- Year/body/trim/VIN **privately**, and what they mean by “rear subframe rust” (repair estimate, failed inspection, mechanic quote, visible holes, seized mounts, sagging or broken suspension).
- Wide photos of **front/rear left/right underfloor**, suspension subframe and mount brackets, **each visible body anchor and bolt entry**, rear inner wheelhouses, sill/jack regions, tank/lines, brake lines and brackets, door apertures and rear cargo floor **both above and below** where available.
- Closeups with a scale, good lighting and orientation. Do **not** infer metal thickness or safe lift load from them.
- Maintenance and corrosion/welding history, former NY inspection rejection notes or previous repair invoices, and any differential/line contamination or visible leaks.
- If safely possible through a shop, physically inspect **body metal, subframe and suspension mount integrity**, not just external subframe cosmetic coating. Obtain written diagnosis and estimate separated into **bolt-on frame/bushing**, **body-mount or longitudinal member repair**, **brake/fuel hydraulics**, and **alignment/testing**.

### Decision gates before spending money

| Observed/claimed condition | Research-only classification | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface rust on nonstructural panel, no underlying damage established | **Unknown until inspected** | Verify beneath panels and at mount points, do not automatically condemn or clear |
| Bolt-on subframe corroded, but **no proof** body anchors sound | **Structural safety evidence insufficient** | Professional lift/inspection and exact OEM bolt-on part applicability |
| Rot/crack/deformation around suspension pickup, frame member or captive-nut metal | **Stop road use / specialist structural assessment** | Collision/body shop familiar with manufacturer repair guidelines, and written cost |
| Rusted or leaking brake line, dripping/pooling fuel, damaged unsupported underbody pipe | **Stop road use / safety repair needed** | Qualified hydraulic/fuel inspection and repair; no ignition source near leaks |
| Jack point severely corroded or an unsafe raised-car condition | **No undercar DIY access** | Professional lift strategy after assessment of structural supports |

None of these rows is a purchase recommendation, certified inspection result, insurance assessment, repair-price estimate, or universal Saab rust thickness threshold.
