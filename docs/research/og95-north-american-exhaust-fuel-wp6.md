# Phase 0 work package 6 — North American exhaust, emissions and EVAP (initial B235 research)

**Research date:** 2026-10-13. **Gate B/A, partial only.** This is the first work-package-6 research tranche for the **original 9-5 (9600), 2008 four-cylinder petrol B235E/R**, motivated by the uninspected 2008 wagon advertised with a clogged catalytic converter. It is **not** a diagnosis, complete North American emissions certification matrix, an approved substitute parts list, or a claim that a specific Saab is healthy.

## What Saab factory material actually establishes

- [Original MY2008 T7 intake/turbo/exhaust diagram](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/trionic-t7-4-cyl/technical-description/system-diagram-intake-turbo-exhaust-4-cyl) depicts a **primary and a main catalytic converter**, then front and rear silencers. The same diagram includes the turbo, EVAP canister and purge valve. This establishes a factory system **architecture**, not the exact US/Canadian VIN-approved exhaust part or the site of a real restriction.
- [B235E/R original front exhaust procedure](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/exhaust-system/adjustment-replacement/front-exhaust-system-with-catalytic-converters-b235e-r) names the front exhaust system with **catalytic converters** and oxygen-sensor connectors. It cautions against flex-section bending beyond **5°** and excessive joint stress; none of this confirms converter flow on a parked car.
- The [MY2008 T7 O2 wiring list](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/trionic-t7-4-cyl/wiring-diagram/trionic-t7-engine-management-system-4-cyl-part-4-4) identifies sensor **592Fa before** and **592Ra after** a converter. The [downstream O2 technical-data excerpt](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/trionic-t7-4-cyl/technical-data/heated-oxygen-sensor-2-after-catalytic-converter-592ra) identifies *Bosch LSF 4.7* and **0–1 V**. These are sensor/electrical facts, **not** manufacturer OE part numbers or pressure measurements. The sensor connector must not be sprayed with cleaner.
- [MY2008 EVAP purging theory](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/trionic-t7-4-cyl/technical-description/purging) documents vapor movement through charcoal canister, purge valve and intake/turbo-side nonreturn valves. It distinguishes purge valve electrical/leak codes **P0444/P0445/P0441** and tank integrity leakage codes **P0456/P0442/P0455**, plus a differential-pressure/closure test path. Option/market equipment must still be established.
- [Saab MY2008 T7 fuel-intake checklist](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/trionic-t7-4-cyl/adjustment-replacement/leak-check-fuel-pressure-flow-capacity-and-adaptation-procedures) explicitly warns that air-leak/fuel-flow symptoms can occur **with no DTC**. It defines a **3 bar fuel-minus-manifold pressure differential** under its instrumented driving procedure and a **maximum 0.6 bar** controlled intake leak test using Saab equipment. **Both require suitable expertise, proper equipment and an already-safe vehicle. Neither is an instruction to run or load-test an unknown non-running project car.**
- [MY2008 T7 P0138 factory diagnosis](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/trionic-t7-4-cyl/fault-diagnosis-diagnostic-trouble-codes/p0138) is **downstream O2 high-voltage electrical diagnosis**, not proof of a clogged catalytic converter. Likewise, the previously captured Saab bulletin **248-2506 explicitly excludes US/Canada**; its running-engine Tech2 supplemental result must not be imported as a North American factory authorization.

## US/Canadian regulatory boundary

[NYSDEC's converter guidance](https://dec.ny.gov/environmental-protection/air-quality/controlling-motor-vehicle-pollution) prohibits **used/recycled/salvaged converter** sale and installation in New York, with CARB-certified replacement obligations applying according to the vehicle's original California/50-state certification and relevant model-year rules. The exact lawful replacement must be established from the **actual car's emissions label, jurisdiction, and compliant new OE/CARB application**. This report does not make a general claim that any B235 converter from a different model year or federal-certified car is legal or physically interchangeable.

## Decision path for the possible $1,200 2008 wagon

1. **Before travel:** obtain actual VIN (privately), underhood emissions decal, seller's diagnosis paperwork, any DTC/freeze-frame printout, original repairs, reason it stopped driving and when it last ran. No proof means the converter is **unverified**.
2. **Structural gate first:** rear subframe and **body mounts/captive nuts**, rear floor, suspension pickups, fuel and brake lines, jacking structure. Severe structural or fuel leaks supersede all exhaust price discussion.
3. **Non-invasive review:** document exhaust completeness, leaks, wiring/connector condition, loose heat shields, previous cuts/welds and damage. Distinguish a catalyst-efficiency code, O2 electric fault, misfire/rich adaptation and measured physical backpressure.
4. **Conditional technician tests:** only if safe, qualified shop records cylinder/engine/oil evidence, controls fuel and intake testing and performs a valid exhaust restriction localization rather than treating P0420/P0138 or a seller label as direct pressure data. Avoid unsafe road/stall testing or exhaust/emissions removal to bypass a fault.
5. **Written cost gate:** independently quote actual legally compliant exhaust repair, any root-cause ignition/fuel/oil work, structural restoration and recovery/tow charges. An untested automatic gearbox remains **mechanically unknown**.
