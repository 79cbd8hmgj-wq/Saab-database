## Why this changes the buying research without producing a winner

1. **There is concrete U.S.-facing retail support for some priority original 9-5 components.** The 9-5 does not lack starter/cooling/ignition options merely because Saab manufacturing ended. A publicly quoted Saab/GM water pump also exists for the second-gen B207 9-3. These **selected parts are not a representative whole-car stock survey**.
2. **U.S. vendor presence is distinct from US/Canada VIN-approved application and actual stock.** The manufacturer [lists authorized US and Canadian online partners](https://saabparts.com/us/affiliated-retail-parts-websites/). A retailer's U.S. storefront or an 'in-stock' product page cannot establish a particular North American Saab VIN's original OE component or guaranteed fulfillment.
3. **One small-service part price cannot determine which whole Saab is more reliable.** T7 cassette, coolant radiator, gear-driven pump and starter are different repairs, with different diagnosis, programming, tooling and labor. For T8, clone-only programming fees require working readable original module and a donor ECU, so cannot be directly compared to buying a complete Saab T7 cassette.
4. **Conditional manufacturer warranties should not be extended to a DIY project.** [Saab Parts U.S. describes 3yr/36k-miles original parts-and-labor coverage](https://saabparts.com/us/why-book-with-us/) only in its official-center service context. Verify individual DIY retailer terms separately.
5. **Delivery and stock volatility are themselves a rebuild risk.** Another retailer-cached listing of 32022255 had two units instead of 20+, and radiator 32019620 has appeared as a Sweden-delivery item as well as the in-stock product page. We therefore store quotations as dated supplier claims and leave stock verification `false`.

## Next meaningful Gate C proof

At the point of examining a real candidate car, read the actual VIN/build, transmission code and stamped installed part numbers, then collect dated comparable dealer quotes for its **top-cost/high-failure systems** (ECU/security, engine/turbo, transmission, suspension/structure and emissions) before adding small-consumable part savings. For the original 9-5 B235, an actual 2008 wagon's structural repair cost and engine/transmission health outrank nominal radiator/starter price. For the B207 9-3, the ECU and original coolant-pump timing/tooling risks must be budgeted, not inferred from aftermarket catalogues.
