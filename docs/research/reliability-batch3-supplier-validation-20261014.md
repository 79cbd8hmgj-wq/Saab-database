## Evidence classification

**Verified:** listed product identity and stated model-family application on named seller pages. **Unverified:** specific VIN fitment, fulfilled stock, complete installed bill, mechanical durability advantage and whole-car reliability.

Buying exposure must include vehicle price, towing, diagnosis, tools, parts, consumables, outsourced labor and contingency. A low replacement-part sticker price is not sufficient to recommend a damaged project vehicle.
