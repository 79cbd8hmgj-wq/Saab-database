## Stronger manufacturer source for the original 9-3

Saab's original [TSB 210-2418 edition 4 (April 2006), 9-3 (9400)](https://www.partsforsaabs.com/info_documents/55561200_93.pdf) states **M99–M03 B205 and B235**, distributed all markets, specifically for a **customer complaint of engine oil leakage**. This original factory-authored PDF is **mirrored by partsforsaabs.com**, not published by that retailer. Bulletin page 1 names crankcase-ventilation renovation kit **55 561 200**. It lists an oil trap, oil filler pipe, nipples, insulation, two hose kits, ties and bushing. Its *conditional* supplementary hoses/check-valve references are:

| Factory reference | Description | Canadian reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `55 560 445` | hose to oil pan | `30566431` |
| `55 560 463` | camshaft-cover hose | `30581025` |
| `93 99 973` | hose including check valve | `30585634` |

Page 3 calls for the **30 14 883 pressure/vacuum pump** to test the small-circuit check valve and states the check-valve arrow direction. This is **specialized factory tooling**, not a guarantee an equivalent test or lift access is available in the driveway. Page 7 gives **0.9 hour historic Saab warranty-operation time** for the complaint procedure. That is not a 2026 labor estimate or realistic DIY-time prediction. The bulletin reserves it for trained technicians and does not authorize working on a structurally unsafe or oil-starved nonrunning car.

The MY1999 inclusion of the original bulletin does **not** create an unsupported 1999 B205 production row. Our pre-existing **7 original 9-3 MY2000–02 automatic B205** groups are a subset of the factory applicability; B204 is not included. Separately, the original [9-5 MY1998–03 B205/B235 bulletin 210-2417](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2001/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/bulletins-si-mi/210-2417-utg-4-oil-leakage-from-engine) is already in earlier research. Neither bulletin makes the kit compulsory for every eligible car.

## Outstanding purchase blockers

1. Obtain actual 9-3/9-5 factory engine and PCV-hardware identification and a Saab Parts/WebEPC VIN-filtered original application record; preserve a full VIN **privately**.
2. Determine which hoses, check valve, pipe, seals, clamps and original PCV revision are fitted; verify whether the vehicle has the bulletin's actual complaint.
3. Obtain manufacturer or seller-confirmed **exact components in the shipped partial/complete box**, particularly `55554444`'s internally inconsistent description/table.
4. Obtain an **actual landed U.S. quote** including taxes, delivery and supplementary hoses; record special-tool access and safe lifting or separately quoted specialist labor. No cost here includes those items.
5. Do **not** infer reduced bearing wear or a known failure-rate reduction merely from a catalogue's promotional wording. Existing internal engine damage is a separate diagnostic/no-rebuild decision.

**Scope and safety:** 9-3 (9440) B207, 9-5 B308E V6, and newer B235 layouts do not inherit early original 9-3 9400 PCV eligibility. Factory service is complaint-conditioned; repair based on actual diagnosis, not statistical assumption or a price listing.
