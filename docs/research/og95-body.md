# OG Saab 9-5 body and chassis supplement

This focused pass adds 25 documented facts from 10 factory-manual mirror pages and five gaps. Its main coverage is MY2005 generic WIS plus limited MY2009 US/CA data.

Dimensions distinguish sedan and wagon, body width and mirror width, unloaded versus gross-weight conditions, and market-specific sources. Generic weight ranges remain ranges. They have no inferred engine, transmission or option mapping. North American ready-for-driving weight is separately identified by its contents.

The MY2009 WIS general page repeats the older body figures and disagrees with the North American owner manual. The WIS geometry snapshot is marked provisional; both source readings remain available. Use the market-specific manual for the corresponding North American profile, then confirm the selected vehicle configuration.

Direct WIS sources establish wheel offset, bolt count/thread and hub centering, front/rear suspension architecture and power-assisted rack-and-pinion steering. Bolt-circle diameter, wheel structural load rating, hard points, spring/damper characteristics and loaded tire radius remain gaps. No aerodynamic parameters were inferred.

The spring/damper identification page links six chassis-code positions to EPC applications; it supports the chassis-identification gap rather than fabricating rates.
