# Phase 0 work package 7 — US/Canada historical recall and maintenance baseline (first tranche)

**Research date: 2026-10-13. Gate A/B: partial.** Priority is an uninspected **MY2008 original 9-5 (9600) petrol B235E/R US/Canada** and identification limitations for US MY2005–06 second-generation 9-3 B207. This report records factory maintenance obligations and historical recall lookup restrictions, **not** an inspected car's service history or an active recall result.

## Recall status: an important old-Saab false negative

- [NHTSA VIN lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) can report unrepaired safety recalls, but it **does not show completed safety recalls, most safety recalls older than 15 years, manufacturer non-safety service campaigns or some not-yet-assigned VINs**. For MY2004–09 Saabs in 2026, **“0 unrepaired recalls” cannot clear historic recalls**.
- [NHTSA's recall FAQ](https://www.nhtsa.gov/resources-investigations-recalls) separately states that **safety recalls do not expire**. The VIN scope, prior repair status, eligibility and current support route must be verified with official manufacturer/servicing channels when possible. **No VIN was searched; no Saab recall was claimed to be unperformed.**
- Retrieve original campaigns from year/model NHTSA history and original Saab bulletins; distinguish **safety recall**, service bulletin, customer satisfaction action and limited **special coverage**. Historical documents do not prove a 2026 free repair, parts availability or customer-specific coverage.

## Original Saab 9-5 MY2008 US/CA service reference

The [factory MY2008 USA/Canada schedule](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/) separately lists the **10,000-mile, 20,000-mile ... 140,000-mile** service milestones and [annual service](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/annual-service). The [10,000-mile checklist](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/10-000-miles-us-ca) includes engine oil+filter, brake fluid and coolant checks, tyre/drive observations, battery, air-conditioning drain, airbag and belt checks. These are **scheduled service work**, not a guarantee that a purchased vehicle received them.

| MY2008 US/CA petrol 9-5 example | Factory printed interval or service action | Source/exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual | Engine oil and filter; inspect fuel system, brake lines, exhaust, suspension, airbag and belts | Annual-service checklist; no prior service inferred |
| Brake fluid | Every **4 years** | Additional-work item; condition and proper hydraulic work matter |
| Cabin air filter | **10,000 miles or 2 years** | Factory also prints 20,000 km (not converted here) |
| Petrol spark plugs | **40,000 miles or 4 years** | Correct ignition/plug configuration requires engine verification |
| Engine air cleaner | **40,000 miles or 4 years** | Petrol baseline, inspect physical housing |
| Automatic transmission fluid | **70,000 miles or 5 years** | Confirm actual gearbox and correct fluid/temperature procedure |
| Auxiliary drive belt | **140,000 miles** | Aged/brittle belt can need earlier inspection even if mileage low |

The [MY2008 petrol SID service-message factory page](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/3-electrical-system/saab-information-display-sid/service/service-message-petrol-driven-cars) documents manual and Tech2 reset and an automatic reset after **60 activations**. A missing warning **does not prove** maintenance was ever performed.

## 9-3 B207 is deliberately separated

The [MY2006 9-3 WW service programme](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2006/1-service/saab-service-ww/technical-description/service-programme) distinguishes **intermediate service** (oil life / distance / time) and **main service** (primarily distance). It contains a questionable printed US/CA kilometre-interval line; the repo **does not import it** as a B207-approved schedule. A US/CA original booklet/year-specific service page is still needed before claiming 9-3 service mileage.

## Pre-purchase evidence requested for a 2008 wagon

1. Capture actual 17-character VIN privately, model-year/engine/gearbox ID, odometer, emissions certification and complete ownership/repair records.
2. Compare historical year/make/model NHTSA recall documents and Saab bulletin/authorized workshop history; enter individual **completion status only with evidence**. A negative VIN search stays inconclusive for older campaigns.
3. Obtain dates/mileage, receipts and part numbers for oil/filter, brake-fluid work, ATF, spark plugs, filters and drive belt. If unknown, budget inspection, not a hypothetical zero cost.
4. Inspect the **rear subframe and body mounting**, plus fuel/brake lines and original exhaust path first. Any leaks/structural damage stop driving or road testing until a qualified evaluation.
5. Keep Gate A partial and Gate B/C not met until vehicle identity, structure and applicable parts evidence are actually obtained.

**Blocked:** individual VIN campaign history; true service records; year/market revisions; MY2005–06 B207 US/CA service documents. Nothing here approves a replacement or verifies a real vehicle's condition.
