# MY2006–08 B235R lubrication, service boundaries and PCV catalogue conflicts

**Research date:** 2026-10-12. This is Phase 0 research, **not** a parts-ordering compatibility engine, consumer oil-purchase advice, a simulator or a repair authorization. Findings are deliberately separated into year-specific original factory manuals, factory WIS summaries, and independent supplier catalogue statements.

## 1. MY2006 four-cylinder petrol lubricating system — primary factory evidence

MY2006 [Saab WIS lubricating-system technical data](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/technical-data/lubricating-system) has now been read in full and independently recorded. Earlier datasets independently document MY2005, MY2007 and MY2009, but the year scope of each entry must stay separate.

| Specification | MY2006 original WIS | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Complete volume with oil cooler/filter | 5.4 L | **Do not** use as routine oil-change refill |
| Service refill incl. filter | 4.0 L | Recheck dipstick, condition and leakage |
| MAX–MIN dipstick | 1.0 L | Not an automatic top-off volume |
| Hot mechanical minimum | 2.5 bar at 2000 rpm, 10W-30 oil, 105 ± 2°C | A controlled hot test; **not** idle spec |
| Oil warning switch | 0.3–0.55 bar | Lamp can stay off even below running-pressure acceptance limit |
| Oil pump reducing valve opening | 3.8 bar | A separate relief value |
| Rotor/housing axial end float | 0.03–0.08 mm | Requires internal inspection |
| Engine oil cooler thermostat | 105 ± 2°C | Not coolant thermostat |

The [MY2006 factory measuring procedure](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/adjustment-replacement/measuring-oil-pressure) locates the mechanical test port at the turbo oil-feed banjo by the oil-filter adapter and names special banjo 8395576 and pressure equipment 8393852. These are procedural tool references, not evidence of a car's pressure or authority to test an unsafe/noisy engine.

The MY2008 petrol *Technical data* [directory](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/technical-data/) contains a Lubricating system entry, but its actual webpage could not be independently retrieved here. **We do not assign the MY2006 numbers to MY2008.** The separately indexed MY2008 **diesel** lubricating system is not relevant to the petrol B235 engine.

## 2. MY2008 United States / Canada 2.3 Turbo Ecopower — factory owner's manual

The original [2008 US/Canada Saab 9-5 owner's manual](https://manualzz.com/doc/53326799/saab-2008-9-5-owner-s-manual) establishes:
- The named 2.3 Turbo Ecopower, **260 hp EEC**, has a **4.0 L** oil-change fill including filter (p.264). Its marketing name is not an engine-stamping decode, so the record is limited to the named car and market, not arbitrarily assigned a B235R calibration.
- For petrol engines, the historical synthetic approval is **GM-LL-A-025**, with Saab Long Life Turbo Oil viscosities **0W-30, 0W-40, 5W-30 or 5W-40** (p.263). A current bottle using the same viscosity is *not* automatically approved. Oil additives are discouraged.
- Original maintenance scheduling distinguishes the SID prompt from **extra oil and filter service at 5,000 miles / 8,000 km** for frequent trips below 5–10 miles, prolonged idling, dust, trailer/roof load, and commercial duty (p.254; independently shown [here](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/746387/saab-2008-9-5.html?page=254)). This is documented preventive **service guidance**, not proof it will yield a Toyota-equivalent lifetime.
- Check oil with engine warm but shut off for 2–5 minutes on level ground; maintain MIN–MAX without overfill or running low (p.194).

The generic [MY2008 WIS service page](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/service/engine-oil-and-oil-filter) explicitly labels **B205E/B235E** in the 4.0 L oil-capacity and **25 Nm** drain plug lines. Thus that webpage is *not* evidence of B235R-specific plug torque, even though the 2008 US/CA owner's manual separately establishes a 4.0 L change fill for 2.3 Turbo Ecopower.

## 3. B235R manual gearbox engine/sump identity

The [MY2006 engine serial code guide](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/technical-data/engine-serial-no) identifies the **R** version at position five as high-pressure turbo, **E** at position six as 9-5 compliance and **A/M** at position seven as transmission type. Its displayed sample contains a **2004** model-year digit, which is illustrative, **not a MY2006 VIN**.

The [MY2007 B235R manual gearbox sump procedure](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2007/2-engine/basic-engine-4-cylinder-petrol/adjustment-replacement/oil-sump-in-car-b235r-manual) is distinct from the generic sump instructions: it describes access panels and a physical sump change tied to engine-number notation **Y062148-**. It contains fastener values for **sump 22 Nm**, **gearbox 47 Nm**, **access plate 7 Nm**; these are *not* engine-oil drain plug torques. A separately titled MY2008 B235R manual sump page is indexed, but full illustrations/installation text are not verified here. Do not attempt to drill/punch an existing vehicle using this summary.

## 4. PCV — vendor diagrams and physical-fitment ambiguity

The [supplier exploded PCV diagram](https://www.esaabparts.com/viewparts.php?section=314781123) is useful for research, but it is **not** the official manufacturer WebEPC. It lists:
- Valve-cover hose **5955919** for 2004–2005 (a supplier's Orio-labelled merchant SKU **33457831**) versus **55355330** for 2006–2009.
- Hose **5961552** in early 2004 up to engine number **4017590**; **5961610** from **4017591 onward**.
- Oil trap **55558696** described as 2004–2005, while that [supplier page](https://www.esaabparts.com/saab/parts/55558696) lists **12755243** under *superseded part numbers*. Independently, the vendor lists aftermarket **32019651** as a **2004–2009** oil-trap separator with Saab **12755243** as an OEM reference. These different year/headline statements are not proof of a full original-equipment supersession chain.
- Valve-equipped breather hose **5955927**, lower hose **55560445**, and trap hose **55557709** as supplier-listed 2004–2009 references.
- ProParts full bundle **213452433** includes aftermarket merchant SKUs, not an official approved manufacturer kit. Promotional 'upgraded' hose materials and current vendor stock statements are not recorded as measured engineering benefits or permanent availability.

All new parts carry `supplier_claim` and `needs_vehicle_verification`; **no OEM supersession, interchange or retrofit approval** is created.
