## Non-negotiable safety screen
**STOP** if load-bearing corrosion, unsafe brake/fuel system, suspected mechanical seizure/oil starvation/hydrolock, or confirmed internal engine/gearbox failure needing excluded rebuild. **Do not jump, crank, drive, or lift on questionable structure merely to diagnose a nonrunner.**

**HOLD** if title/VIN, rust underneath, cold engine health, gearbox health, or seller's repair explanation is unverified.

**FINALIST (not yet purchased)** if apparently sound structure, legal papers, complete unique components, known history and evidence of manageable external faults. Obtain a competent independent pre-purchase inspection.

## Three targeted questions for a 2011
1. Has it ever been **difficult to start cold but fine hot**, and was a cold compression/leakdown performed? ([Saab Parts technical advisory](https://saabparts.com/us/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/2023/05/Hard-Starting-Cold-With-Engine-Stall-Misfire-and-Low-Compression-Starts-and-Runs-Normally-When-Hot-07-11-9-3-11-9-5.pdf))
2. Have the **timing chain, balance-shaft drive, cooling, oil pressure or AF40** been professionally diagnosed or repaired? If so, what tests and exact part numbers? ([Saab MY2011 WIS](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9650/2011/2-engine/basic-engine-2-0-l-petrol/fault-diagnosis-fault-symptoms/timing-chain-check))
3. Are the **headlamps, bumpers, body electronics and keys complete and working**? Some individual retail parts are unavailable; source by exact VIN, option and condition. ([LH halogen headlamp listing](https://www.esaabparts.com/saab/parts/12775748))

This sequence is project inspection judgment linked to the existing factory evidence;
it is not a substitute for the complete WIS tests. Use the

### 01. Identity and legal documents

- Privately match full VIN on title, body and manufacturer labels; seller authority and liens.
- Verify original US 2011 Turbo4/Premium sedan, FWD and automatic; verify factory options rather than trim badge.
- Photograph engine and transmission identifiers, case tags and prior swap invoices privately.

**STOP:** Title/VIN tampering or unlawful transaction; confirmed excluded configuration is outside this project.

**HOLD:** missing title/ownership proof, mismatched VIN, uncertain engine/transmission identity.

- Last ran/moved dates, reason parked, exact seller fault description and invoices.

- Overnight ambient/ECT/IAT and whether seller pre-warmed engine.

## FINALIST gate and economic decision

FINALIST requires matched documents/configuration, a competent structural and safety
inspection, sufficient cold engine and transmission evidence, working security/keys,
and an obtainable repair route for every essential rare assembly. The essential-cost
worksheet must include parts, required hardware/seals, fluids, freight, tax/core,
tools, programming, specialist work, towing, title/inspection and an explicitly
estimated contingency. An unavailable listing or unknown charge is not zero.

Confirmed internal engine/gearbox work outside the owner's accepted objective is STOP.
A complete external assembly swap is a separate, potentially expensive decision;
its technical possibility does not establish economic feasibility. A nonrunner with
an untested transmission remains HOLD even if the engine fault sounds inexpensive.
No road test until structure, tires, brakes, restraints and legal operation are
competently assessed. FINALIST authorizes more inspection and pricing, not purchase
or a statement that the car is safe.
