**Use alongside:** [13-case purchase/repair engineering batch](ng93-b207-powertrain-engineering-batch-20261014.md). ## Step 1. Before visiting the seller (ask for evidence)

- [ ] Real VIN and body/engine/gearbox identity; do not rely on seller's “2005 turbo” description.
- [ ] Cold start video from a genuinely overnight-cold engine; **warm start cannot clear B207 inlet-valve wear**.
- [ ] Photos of engine compartment coolant hoses, underbody **both** subframes and mounts, spring seats, brake and fuel lines.
- [ ] Copies of cylinder-head/valve invoices showing whether **all eight inlet valves** were replaced and how cylinder-head seats were inspected.
- [ ] Copies of early **FA57** cooler connection correction, ATF contamination history and gearbox repair invoices; ask whether any cooler/ATF mixing was ever found.
- [ ] Readout or written list of **T8, TCM, CIM, ISM/SCL** codes; ask for **both working programmed keys**.
- [ ] Engine/turbo/PCV/oil change records; no claimed “new engine” without identifiable paperwork.
- [ ] Old recall campaign history and any outstanding VIN campaigns checked separately; an open-recall-only search **does not** prove completed work.
- [ ] Seller grants safe, independent inspection; no unsafe road test to diagnose severe mechanical/safety faults.

## Step 2. At the car: stop conditions come first

| Safety or major-cost finding | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Brake/fuel line leaks, load-bearing rust or cracked body-to-subframe attachment | **Do not drive**; independent repair-cost/safety opinion before buying |
| Fractured front coil spring, visibly unstable strut seat or unsafe steering | **Do not drive**; verify spring correction |
| Strong mechanical engine knock, dangerously low measured oil pressure or severe uncontrolled overheat | **Do not drive**; price as major mechanical work only |
| Technician confirms coolant in early FA57 ATF / uncontrolled gearbox slipping | **Do not drive**; engine/gearbox build and full transmission/cooler quote needed |
| No confirmed engine or transmission identity | No compatibility, parts, replacement or budget assumption until resolved |

When the car is *safe* to continue:
- [ ] Genuinely cold engine starts without extended cranking, recurring miss, stall or unusual persistent timing noise.
- [ ] Engine oil/coolant appear uncontaminated, correct levels verified **without opening hot cooling system**; source of leaks independently checked.
- [ ] Engine and transmission-specific codes captured **before clearing**; freeze-frame and module identity saved.
- [ ] Charge-air piping/hoses and PCV routing physically secure; no conclusion about turbo shaft from underboost code alone.
- [ ] Radiator/hoses/pump/thermostat/fans show no uncontrolled leak/overheat, based on authorized safe operating tests.
- [ ] Check ISM OFF/key-removal, remote lock/unlock, CIM and steering lock, with **both keys**.
- [ ] Structurally examine both subframes, body mounts/pickup metal, brake/fuel lines, and 2003–06 front coil lower spring seats.
- [ ] If appropriate, qualified cold compression/leak-down and hot comparison: measured method/cylinder values attached.
- [ ] If appropriate, specialist FA57 ATF and radiator cooler test: sample/temperature/gearbox suffix attached.
- [ ] If the car is roadworthy, observe cold/hot gearbox shift quality without deliberately abusing damaged parts.

## Step 3. Evidence required before accepting “already fixed”

| Seller assertion | Minimum acceptable supporting evidence |
| --- | --- |
| “Valves were upgraded” | Dated invoice showing valve **part numbers**, quantity, seat/machine work and proper **post-repair cold measurements** |
| “Later year doesn't have valve problems” | Year-only claim insufficient: actual production hardware/revision and cold tests required |
| “Transmission problem was just radiator seals” | Identify VIN/FA57 suffix; establish whether **ATF was ever contaminated** and repair/cooler action carried out |
| “ECU is bad because P0606” | Capture all codes and voltages; original **TA248-0336** software false-positive diagnostic branch considered |
| “New computer installed” | Document exact donor hardware, functional security pairing/programming, adaptation and diagnostic success |
| “New ignition switch” | Full switch or **32021815 partial kit**? Condition of reused internal black sliding mechanism |
| “Key problem is always CIM” | Compare factory **365-2568** branch for individual remote, CIM software, ISM, SCL and real VIN |
| “Water pump is easy and upgraded” | Actual gear-driven B207 WIS tool/part check (**12630084**, tool **83 96 103**); evidence for better lifespan absent |
| “Front suspension was done” | Both correct springs, lower seats and zinc plates if factory correction applies; no assumption old special coverage still pays |
| “Rust is just the removable subframe” | Professional examination of **body mounting points**, line routing and repair geometry, not only the component |
