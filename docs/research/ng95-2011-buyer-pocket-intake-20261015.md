# 2011 Saab 9-5 (650/9650) — private lot inspection pocket card

Use with the [2011 NG 9-5 evidence dossier](ng95-2011-purchase-reliability-baseline-20261015.md). Focus: the U.S. Turbo4/Turbo4 Premium FWD six-speed automatic; 2.8 V6 XWD cars require a separate assessment. Do **not** post full VINs, title photos, seller contact details or private sale records in public GitHub.

## At the car — capture first, decide later

- Private visit ID N95-___; seller asking price; mileage; date last running and last driving; actual reason parked; keys; title/brands/liens; towing needs.
- Privately photograph 17-character VIN. For **MY2011** WIS: digit 7 **A = 6AT FWD**, **B = 6AT XWD**; digit 8 **Z = A20NFT/LHU 2.0T gasoline**, **J = A28NER/LAU V6**; digit 10 **B = 2011**. Photograph physical engine and transmission identification. WIS year-specific identity alone does not prove installed hardware.
- Photos with permission: front/side/rear, wheels/arches, lamps and bumpers, dash/odometer and warnings, engine bay and labels, safe external view of body sills, rails, suspension/body mounting points, brake/fuel lines, harness integrity and visible leaks.
- Record seller's *exact* diagnosis, supporting invoice/tests, whether cold-start issues were observed, and whether a complete parts donor is included.

## High-impact questions specific to Turbo4 automatic

| System | Ask / inspect | Hold or stop trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A20NFT cold compression | Overnight-cold hard start, stalls or P0300–P0304; genuinely cold compression/leakdown proof? | Runs fine warm is **not** clearance: factory advisory includes 2011 A20NFT valve-face wear |
| Lubrication and chains | Oil warnings, knocking, rattle, pump/pickup/timing work; oil-service records? | Suspected seizure/starvation = **no test start**, request qualified mechanical checks |
| Fuel system | No-start/misfire; ECM and pump-controller codes and readings? | Cannot assume the HP pump is defective from one code |
| Cooling | Leaks, coolant level messages, relay/fan operation, overheating, radiator/ATF cross-contamination? | Persistent overheat or fluid mixing needs professional work |
| AF40 six-speed | Cold/hot shift behavior only when safe, ATF service proof, case ID and TCM fault scan? | Confirmed internal slipping/damage or no dependable gearbox evidence = HOLD/STOP |
| Structural | Rear support **and body attachments**, front supports, sills, brake and fuel lines? | Load-bearing rust and unsafe support locations = STOP |
| Model-specific parts | All lamps, bumper pieces, glazing, keys, ECU/BCM modules present? | Missing major 650-only part = HOLD until real supplier/price confirmed |

A visual rust pass and dealer's explanation cannot prove structural safety. No crawling under corroded/unsupported cars; no uncontrolled jump-starting or driving stored cars. Arrange daylight and qualified lift/mechanical inspection for finalists.

## Outcome

**FINALIST**: correct identity, complete expensive components, structurally promising, legal paperwork and *credible* evidence engine/gearbox can be kept without internal rebuild. **HOLD**: unknown cold compression, unknown automatic or rust, no title/VIN, untested no-start, unpriced scarce parts. **STOP**: dangerous structure/brake/fuel, demonstrably severe engine/trans damage beyond objective, or no legitimate transfer documentation.

For father's **Turbo6 or Aero XWD**, capture photos/VIN/title; **do not transfer the Turbo4-specific intake valve, engine chain or FWD support repair advice**.
