# Phase 0 work package 7 supplementary primary recall notices and conflicting ATF interval

**Research date:** 2026-10-13. This is a **narrow addendum** to the already-merged [work-package-7 US/Canada maintenance and historic lookup baseline](priority-usca-service-recall-baseline-wp7.md). Do not use it as an individual VIN service report or an approved replacement catalogue.

## Actual original US safety-campaign source records

- [NHTSA GM 16V-063 filing](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2016/RCLRPT-16V063-7371.PDF) confirms **2006–09 original 9-5** and **2006–11 second-gen 9-3**, US, with a Takata driver inflator recall. It describes an authorized replacement of the driver inflator, **not** a generic donor part and not proof of current outstanding status. Do not extrapolate to MY2005 9-3.
- [NHTSA 08V-160 acknowledgment](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2008/RCAK-08V160-6768.pdf) cites **MY2002–08 9-5** A-pillar interior occupant protection; the separately filed [November 2008 Saab owner letter](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2008/RCONL-08V160-4604.pdf) addresses **certain MY2006–08 9-5** owners and describes revised energy-absorbing trim. Preserve both dated scopes; do not relabel the problem as a head-restraint failure merely because the administrative component category names seats.
- [GM 14V-318 Part 573 notice](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2014/RCDNN-14V318-3795P.pdf) specifically covers **9-3 convertibles MY2004–11** with driver belt retractor cable defect and recalls the driver-side retractor. Our first-priority MY2005–06 index captures the two years **and convertible only**. GM separately describes passenger-side retractor *special coverage*. It is not interchangeable with the driver safety-recall obligation or evidence of current coverage.

NHTSA's [current VIN lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) omits most recalls older than 15 years and previously completed campaign work. **A 0-result VIN query cannot confirm historic 9-5/9-3 completion.** No actual VIN was entered; a historical legal remedy does not prove a 2026 shop can obtain hardware on demand.

## Two conflicting original US/CA MY2008 9-5 automatic-fluid milestones

- [2008 Saab US/CA 60,000-mile checklist](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/60000-miles-us-ca) explicitly names **automatic gearbox fluid change at the 60,000-mile service**.
- The previously recorded [MY2008 Saab US/CA annual service](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/annual-service) lists automatic fluid **70,000 miles or 5 years** under additional work.

Both are manufacturer-authored WIS content for the same US/CA model-year folder, yet are nonidentical. We preserve a separately documented issue, do not choose a favorite, and require the original printed service booklet, WIS edition/errata and actual FA57/automatic identity before adopting a definitive interpretation. This matters for a cheap automatic wagon whose maintenance history is unknown.

The additional [60,000-mile service](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/60000-miles-us-ca) and [100,000-mile US/CA checklist](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/100000-miles-us-ca) explicitly call for fuel tank/system and brake line/hose inspection; the [programme choice sheet](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-eu/technical-description/choice-of-service-programme) separates US/CA, EU and WW service. None can establish that the structurally rusty advertised wagon is safe, compliant or maintained.

## What remains blocked

These safety findings are independent of the missing VIN-specific OEM parts approval.
