# Original Saab 9-5 B205/B235 buyer inspection — 1998–2009 factory-source range

**For:** US/CA original 9-5 (9600) four-cylinder petrol, especially 1999–2009 U.S. FWD automatic sedan/SportCombi. The 1998 year in some bulletins is NOT proof of U.S. production. **Do not apply to B308E V6, 9-3 B207, or new-generation 9-5.** Take the full VIN privately; do not commit seller identity or full VIN to a public repo. ## STOP: do not drive or crank without suitable professional judgment

- [ ] Unsafe subframe, suspension attachment or chassis/jacking metal, especially rear wagon captive nuts and floor — **O95-16**
- [ ] Leaking/rotted brake line, fuel hose or brackets; abnormal braking — **O95-17**
- [ ] Strong bottom-end knock, critical oil-pressure warning or suspected pickup blockage; do not risk further bearing damage — **O95-01 / O95-02**
- [ ] Severe uncontrolled overheating, oil/coolant contamination or suspected head distortion — **O95-14**
- [ ] Uncontrolled slipping/no forward drive in confirmed FA57/AF30; do not heat by stall test — **O95-11 / O95-12**

## When car is safe to inspect

- [ ] Genuine overnight-cold start and timing/knock record without prolonged cranking
- [ ] Mechanical oil-pressure test if diagnostically justified with exact year procedure and qualified operator; warning switch alone not enough
- [ ] Examine PCV routing and invoice: early 55561200, MY2004–05 *manual-only* 15433, selected MY2004–05 15435 **kept distinct**
- [ ] Capture raw T7/TCM/TWICE codes and freeze-frame BEFORE reset, plus supported cranking-RPM, ignition feed and grounds
- [ ] Confirm **black T7** ignition cassette with correct part identity; never substitute red T5
- [ ] Evaluate turbo hoses, bypass valve and actuator before condemning turbo
- [ ] Cold coolant and leak/pressure assessment, radiator automatic cooler connections, fans and thermostat; do not open hot cap
- [ ] For roadworthy car only, compare cold/hot gear engagement and shifts under normal operation, without forced test/stall
- [ ] Inspect 9-5 rear subframe, *body mount* captive nuts, longitudinal rails, wagon rear floor, rear arches, sills and underbody hardlines with professional lift
- [ ] Measure actual rotor/caliper setup and inspect brake pipes/parking brake shoes; do not use conflicting fastener instruction blindly
- [ ] Check catalytic restriction only with qualified evidence and locate stage; document emissions certification and legal new part
