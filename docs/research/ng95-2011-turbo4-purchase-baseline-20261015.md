# Second-generation Saab 9-5 (650 / WIS 9650) — U.S. 2011 purchase and reliability baseline
**Research date:** 2026-10-15. **Status:** focused evidence intake; not a reliability ranking, VIN-approved replacement list, or verified repair-cost estimate.

## Scope and separation
This dossier targets the **U.S. 2011 new-generation 9-5 Turbo4 / Turbo4 Premium sedan, 2.0L turbo, factory FWD, six-speed automatic** as a prospective project-car purchase. The 2010 launch cars, AWD/XWD Turbo6/Aero, V6, BioPower, European diesels, and 9-3 B207 are **not automatically covered**. The newer 650 / 9650 platform is **not** the original 9600 B235 9-5. Supplementary 2011 AWD/V6 research is a separate backlog item.

The compiled eSaabParts U.S. production register reconciled in [production analysis](../us-production-catalogue-analysis.md) contains one qualifying 2011 FWD automatic new-9-5 group; this is a *compiled-source grouping*, not a VIN certification, supply assessment or measured failure rate.

## 1. Factory identification
[Saab MY2011 WIS VIN breakdown](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9650/2011/0-general/general/technical-description/vehicle-engine-and-transmission-id-and-vin-location-derivative-and-usage) defines:
- VIN character **10 = B** for MY2011.
- Character **7 = A** six-speed automatic **FWD**, while **B** is six-speed automatic XWD. Do not assume every 2011 9-5 is FWD.
- Character **8 = Z** 2.0 T **A20NFT/LHU**, whereas **R** denotes an A20NFT/LHU *BioPower* configuration in the factory chart. Do not infer U.S. BioPower retail availability from the multinational factory chart.
- Legal VIN visible at lower windshield; physical engine and AF40 case identifications still required before parts purchase.
- A factory 2.0T code does **not** imply the 9-3 B207 engine is mechanically interchangeable with it.

The [factory family gearbox evidence](../us-automatic-gearbox-factory-crosscheck.md) identifies **AF40** automatic for new 9-5, with individual gearbox case, software and donor compatibility still unresolved.

## 3. Additional major purchase risks that require new source work
- **Crash and structural/rust:** front/rear cradles, body anchorages, rocker/jacking areas, brake/fuel lines, evidence of unrepaired collisions. Document safe external observations; professionally inspect support points. The 9600 rear-subframe rust decision cannot be transplanted unchanged.
- **Electronic network / immobilizer / keys / modules:** confirm programmed working keys, start authorization, display/control functions, water intrusion and diagnostic communication. A generic OBD reader cannot rule out proprietary module faults. No donor module or software cloning approved here.
- **Cooling, turbo, fuel, HVAC:** inspect leaks, pressure, heat management and service invoices; year-specific component inventory and validated replacements remain outstanding.
- **Airbag/recall status:** obtain full VIN privately and check both NHTSA and Saab historical campaigns; absence from current >15-year open-recall lookup is not historical clearance.
- **Very low-volume body-specific parts:** check lamps, bumper, grille, interior electronics, glass, exterior trim and electrical modules; always record date, exact SKU and seller deliverability instead of saying "parts unavailable" generally.

## 4. Lot visit: required evidence before considering one for purchase
1. **VIN privately**, title/VIN match, actual FWD automatic and Turbo4 identity, odometer, asking price, keys, completeness.
2. **Last ran**, last driven, stored exposure, exact seller hypothesis and diagnostic/test evidence. Record uncertainties rather than "just a sensor".
3. Ground-level inspection and photos of **load-bearing rust, subframe/body mounts, jack points, brake/fuel lines**, leaks, collision, missing parts. **No crawling under unsupported vehicles or unsafe lifting.**
4. **Overnight cold-start proof** where mechanically safe; suspicious misfire/no-start needs qualified cold compression/leak-down, not seller reassurance.
5. **Oil pressure/engine noise/timing/ATF** professionally assess if symptoms exist. No unsafe jump/crank/road-test of long-stored car.
6. Working keys and ECM/TCM/body/security module diagnostic records **without clearing codes**.
7. Price **VIN-correct** parts, towing, diagnostic labor, tool/programming needs, emissions, structural remediation and New York registration before purchase.
8. Classify only **STOP** (unsafe structure, known internal drivetrain disaster, prohibited paperwork), **HOLD** (insufficient evidence), **FINALIST** (promising but still needs professional inspection). No automatic approval.
