# MY2006–2008 North American OG 9-5 specification supplement

Research date: 2026-10-12.

## Scope and evidence strength

This supplement adds **7 manufacturer-authored source references, 25 records and 6 explicit gaps** in `data/datasets/og95-2006-2008.json`. The MY2008 US owner-manual specification pages and MY2007/MY2008 generic WIS engine tables were read in full. The relevant MY2006 and MY2007 US owner-manual entries were accessible in indexed excerpts only, so those records retain provisional/source-excerpt limitations. The MY2006 camshaft data is generic WIS engine-family context.

### Why these boundaries matter

The MY2007 and MY2008 North American manuals report 260 hp EEC for the 2.3 Turbo, while generic WIS B235R columns report 250 hp EEC. These are **separate sourced statements**, not an arithmetic conflict to fix by averaging. Production, software calibration, market and rating conventions must be established before comparing them. Some manual excerpts are still awaiting full page verification.

Service oil-change volumes, gearbox oil capacity and generic dry-fill specifications must remain separate. Similarly, owner's-manual disc diameter alone cannot authorize a rotor purchase without actual rotor/caliper/carrier identification.
