# 9-5 B235 replacement electrics: AS-PL/Opel countercheck to 9-3 sourcing hypothesis

**Research date: 2026-10-13.** This second manufacturer-sourced pass corrects comparison bias after the 9-3 B207 and 9-5 B235 alternator/starter study. We do **not** rank Saab models from source totals or infer used Opel donor suitability.

## Supplier manufacturer's evidence

- **Original 9-5 B235R starter:** [AS-PL S0034](https://as-pl.com/en/p/S0034), [manufacturer applications](https://as-pl.com/en/tpu/S0034). The product is a 12 V, **1.4 kW**, clockwise, **9-tooth**, 2-mounting-hole starter. The catalogue includes original 9-5 B235R entries through 2009 and unrelated Opel/Vauxhall applications. It includes Saab OE reference numbers. **An aftermarket S0034 fitting multiple marques does not mean a particular used Opel original starter is interchangeable.**
- **Another 9-5 alternator family:** [AS-PL A0219](https://as-pl.com/en/p/A0219), [manufacturer applications](https://as-pl.com/sr/tpu/A0219/2). Manufacturer specifies **12 V, 120 A, 6 grooves and PL_9104**; the reference list includes Saab and Opel numbers and B235R 9-5 2006–09 variants. This is supplier data, not actual fitted Saab VIN confirmation.
- **Previously recorded alternative:** [AS-PL A0319](https://as-pl.com/en/p/A0319) has **130 A, 6 grooves and PL_9105**. A0219 and A0319 cannot be treated as interchangeable or as upgrade levels without original 9-5 application, regulator and pulley geometry verification.

## Correcting our model-selection interpretation

**Both priority Saabs have cross-brand aftermarket starter/charging candidates**, in addition to genuine and Saab-specific support. A 9-3 B207 may still offer wider access for selected replacements, but the *current number of cross-brand source rows is not a valid proof* of longer service life, cheaper total repairs or greater resilience. Our previous 9-5 starter gap was due to unequal research coverage; this tranche closes the most basic catalogue-evidence part of it.

The highest priority unanswered questions remain original S0034/A0219/A0319 option and gearbox scope, **U.S. delivered costs**, component durability, Trionic 7 vs 8 module repair and programming, drivetrain risks, and actual body mounting corrosion. For any particular non-running project car, inspection and severe repair exposure can dominate small-component sourcing savings.
