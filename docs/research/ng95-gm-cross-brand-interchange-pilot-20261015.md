## Crucial rejection: contaminated transmission controller interchange claim

A [2026 third-party Go-Parts article](https://www.go-parts.com/garage/transmission-control-module-buick-regal-saab-9-5-2011-2013) claims a **GM 6T40/6T45 TEHCM** from a Regal/Cruze is interchangeable with a **2011 9-5**. That article is internally inconsistent with the [original Saab MY2011 AF40/Aisin identification](../us-automatic-gearbox-factory-crosscheck.md). **Mark this TCM interchange UNSUPPORTED / do not purchase**. A GM 6T40 module/valve body must not be substituted into an Aisin AF40 because both are GM-era six-speed applications. Require the actual 9-5 transmission plate, control-unit hardware and part/supplier identifiers and original Saab EPC/WIS programming path.

A [used transmission seller](https://www.ebay.com/itm/305142227644) does list Buick Regal and Saab 9-5 with **A6-AF40 / MDK** and interchange 400-51825, but the listing also presents implausible V6 9-5 rows as a 2.0L FWD unit. **Use only as search lead**; reject the erroneous broad fitment, verify year, drivetrain, physical case tag, converter and calibration, **not a drop-in approval**.

## VIN data conflict worth preserving

The project's [Saab WIS MY2011 VIN technical description](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9650/2011/0-general/general/technical-description/vehicle-engine-and-transmission-id-and-vin-location-derivative-and-usage) records **position 8 Z** for 2.0T A20NFT/LHU and **R** for its BioPower identity. Some salvage interchange sellers describe the LHU as **VIN R at eighth digit** (example [used ECM](https://www.gitpart.com/parts/2011/Saab/SAAB%209-5/engine-motor-control-module/GO5L9w8dcQ)). **Do not silently override original WIS from a generic Hollander-style listing**. Obtain original exact U.S. VIN bulletin and real vehicle tag; mark discrepancy unresolved until verified.

## Research consequence

Cross-brand OEM-family parts **do exist as credible leads** for the new 9-5, particularly cooling and chassis consumables, while crash/body trim, lighting and immobilizer modules still pose potentially high unique supply risks. **No reliability upgrade has been proved**: sharing a number establishes a sourcing path, not longer life. Record OEM provenance vs supplier listing and quote U.S.-delivered prices separately for each inspected car.

**Next gates:** exact 650 Turbo4 EPC diagram/GM original number for each listed candidate; old and new directed supersession; fitment for FWD vs XWD and brake/suspension/market package; electrical protocol/software; manufacturer OEM and material/durability proof; supplier stock/arrival and true install costs. Never infer GM shared platform => universal swap or Saab brand => unique part.
