# B207 intake valves: what this means when buying or repairing a Saab

**Practical conclusion:** A warm-running 9-3 is not enough to clear this problem.
A properly documented valve repair is more useful than assuming a later model
year received better valves. A car with demonstrated valve sealing loss needs
substantial cylinder-head work; treat it accordingly when deciding whether it
fits the owner's preference to avoid internal engine repairs.

This pass reuses `corrections-issue-011`, `hcdiag-issue-002`,
`hcdiag-fact-023` through `026`, and `corrections-part-015`.
Those already record the failure, cold testing and eight-valve repair reference.
New evidence is the factory head-service procedure, official GM service-part
offering, and three separately sourced retailer price/availability displays.
The [structured supplement](../../data/datasets/b207-intake-valve-repair-path-20261014.json)
contains the new findings. No additional fault identity is invented.

## What fails, and which cars need attention?

The saved Saab Parts advisory separates 2007–08 B207R from its later 2009–11
B207 update and excludes B284. It also separately mentions the 2011 new 9-5
A20NFT; that does not make B207 and A20NFT identical engines.

[AERA's original technical note](https://www.engineprofessional.com/TB/TB011117-1.pdf)
describes intake-valve face erosion/cupping and cold compression loss, with
head removal and replacement of all eight inlet valves as its repair path.
Its broader 2003–11 discussion is not a factory affected-production boundary.
Its aftermarket no-repeat-failure statement lacks a denominator and follow-up
period. We cannot turn it into guaranteed durability or a failure percentage.

The exact original valve fitted to an unknown car remains unidentified. The
stored candidate numbers 12786696 and 55192156 do not establish an original
production sequence. Nor have we established the precise alloy/heat-treatment
defect or a factory VIN/build cutoff at which ordinary petrol B207 production
became immune. A service replacement catalogue is not proof of those claims.

## Is there a better repair component?

The existing Saab support advisory identifies BioPower valve **55563529**.
[GM's own parts channel](https://parts.gmparts.com/product/gm-genuine-parts-engine-intake-valve-55563529)
still offers that exact service number, one valve per package. This is a real
additional sourcing channel for the specified service number, not permission
to transplant a GM cylinder head or engine into a Saab.

Genuine 55563529 is the source-supported repair route to investigate first.
Its exact application must match the engine being repaired. The TRW option
below is a lower-priced aftermarket candidate; its seller's OE-supplier label
does not prove it outlasts the genuine valve. No controlled comparative lifespan
test was retrieved.

## How difficult is the repair?

The [MY2008 factory head procedure](https://saabwisonline.com/9-3-9440/2008/2-engine/4-cyl-petrol-e85/basic-engine/adjustment-replacement/cylinder-head)
retains the block in the car while removing the head. It involves timing setup,
engine support and variant-specific cam tools. The supplement records its
renewal items. This supports an **advanced DIY removal/refitting plus competent
head-shop work** assessment, not a beginner repair or a priced labor allowance.

Head-only repair does not require rebuilding a healthy bottom end. It is still
internal engine work. Other causes of compression loss must be ruled out before
assuming eight replacement valves will solve the whole car.

## What improves our buying decision now?

- Request an overnight-cold start and correctly scoped cold compression/leak-down
  results; warm idle, fresh coils or a code-free scan alone do not clear valve wear.
- For a reportedly repaired car, seek an invoice identifying the valves, head-shop
  work and post-repair test results. An unknown used head may carry the same concern.
- A confirmed valve-loss car remains outside an easy-project assumption until the
  actual repair is priced. No prophylactic teardown of a healthy engine is justified
  by these sources alone.

There is a specific repair and several present catalogue offers to investigate.
What remains unknown is original installed revision, a safe later-production
cutoff, comparative replacement lifespan and a complete local repair price.
