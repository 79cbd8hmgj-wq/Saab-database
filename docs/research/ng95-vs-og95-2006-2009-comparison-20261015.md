# 2006–2009 9-5 automatic versus 2011 Turbo4

Research date: 2026-10-15. Partial evidence; no vehicle scoring or comparative failure-rate claim.

Machine-readable source: [comparison dossier](../../data/engineering-dossiers/ng95-vs-og95-2006-2009-comparison-20261015.json).

## Scope

Original U.S.-market 2006–2009 Saab 9-5 B235 four-cylinder, FWD, factory FA57 automatic; sedan comparison. Equipment and VIN restrictions remain necessary.

Original U.S.-market 2011 second-generation 9-5 Turbo4/Turbo4 Premium sedan, A20NFT/LHU, FWD, AF40 automatic.

## Repair and purchase differences

### CMP-01 — Fuel injection

**2006–2009:** B235 port injection; injectors discharge before intake valves.

**2011:** LHU direct injection adds a mechanical high-pressure pump and high-pressure rail. Rail service requires a new high-pressure pipe.

**Practical implication (engineering inference):** The B235 avoids this particular high-pressure pump/pipe repair dependency. Diagnose both tank supply and high-pressure side on the LHU.

**Unknown:** Matched injector and pump repair quotes; comparative frequency unknown.

### CMP-02 — Water pump

**2006–2009:** 2006/2007 four-cylinder WIS removes the accessory Poly-V belt from the pump. Access still involves intake, steering-pump and pipe work.

**Practical implication (engineering inference):** The LHU adds chain-retention tooling and risk from incorrect sprocket handling. Neither job should be priced as a bare pump alone.

**Practical implication (engineering inference):** Require a genuine cold start; a warmed-up demonstration cannot resolve the LHU concern. Cold compression/leakdown can expose work beyond an external-assembly restoration.

## Candidate decisions

- **STOP:** Confirmed major engine/transmission rebuild need outside the stated objective; unsafe structural damage; unresolved critical safety defect that cannot be responsibly repaired within scope.
- **HOLD:** No true cold test, missing keys, missing module communication, unverified gearbox operation, unknown structural condition, or missing rare part without a credible supply path.
- **FINALIST:** Configuration and ownership verified, structure inspected, cold engine and transmission evidence obtained, security functional, and a written repair/parts/programming budget is feasible. This is not a safety or purchase approval.

## Limits

- No measured comparative failure frequency, expected lifespan, reliability score or model-year winner has been established.
- Observed prices are parts-only advertised USD offers, not delivered stock or installed repair quotes. Tax, shipping, cores, seals, fluids, tools, programming and labor remain unpriced unless separately documented.
- A simpler service design can reduce the work in a particular repair; it does not establish a lower fleet failure rate.
- Evidence for one WIS year or equipment version is not blanket verification of every 2006–2009 build. Confirm VIN, engine, gearbox and options.
- Historical WIS programming procedures do not verify present-day tool subscriptions or local specialist access.
