# Phase 0 work package 7 — priority US recalls and 2008 9-5 original service baseline

**Research date 2026-10-13. Gate A/B progress, not complete.** This tranche addresses first-generation 9-5 MY2008 US/Canada maintenance, three explicitly scoped historical **US** recall campaigns relevant to our priority 9-5 MY2004–09 and NG9-3 MY2005–06 shopping range, and a conservative actual-VIN evidence request. **No vehicle, campaign completion, approved present-day remedy part, or service-provider stock has been inspected.**

## Factory 9-5 US/Canada MY2008 maintenance

The [original Saab US/CA annual schedule](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/annual-service), [10k service](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/10-000-miles-us-ca), [60k service](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/60000-miles-us-ca) and [100k checklist](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/100000-miles-us-ca) describe different mileages and temporal triggers; not one generic European recommendation.

| Item | MY2008 original US/CA documented trigger | Important scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engine oil/filter | 10,000-mile service and annual checklist | No proof prior services were completed |
| Brake fluid | Every 4 years | Hydraulic inspection is separate |
| Cabin filter | 10,000 miles or 2 years | Historical service entry |
| Spark plugs (petrol) | 40,000 miles or 4 years | B235 candidate, exact plug fit still separate |
| Engine air filter | 40,000 miles or 4 years | Hardware compatibility not established |
| Automatic transmission fluid | **Annual-work list: 70,000 miles or 5 years** | **CONFLICT:** standalone **60,000-mile checklist includes ATF change** |
| Auxiliary drive belt | 140,000-mile annual-list replacement | Examine belt/tensioner condition; no belt-part authorization |

**Preserve the ATF contradiction** as two source-scoped records and an unresolved issue until a true printed US/CA owner booklet, gearbox family and factory WIS revision can reconcile it. Never assume 60k *means* the 70k schedule is erroneous, or claim 70k overwrites a printed 60k service item. The [US/CA annual checklist](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-us-ca/service/annual-service) also calls for examining underbody exhaust hangers, brake pipes, fuel lines/tank, suspension and safety restraints. For the rusted, possibly clogged-cat **2008 9-5 wagon**, inspection now matters more than a maintenance list: structural safety, fuel leakage and gearbox health are still unproven. The [factory service programme selection](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2008/1-service/saab-service-eu/technical-description/choice-of-service-programme) distinguishes EU/WW/US/CA; do not import an EU-only mileage into North American records by assumption.

## Primary NHTSA recalls: archived campaigns, not VIN-level completion

1. **NHTSA 16V-063 / GM 28810–28811:** official [2016 Part 573 report](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2016/RCLRPT-16V063-7371.PDF) identifies US-market **MY2006–09 original 9-5** and **MY2006–11 second-generation 9-3** driver inflators. Remedy listed is professional driver airbag inflator replacement. **Do not apply to MY2005 9-3** without separate evidence. Model-year inclusion is not proof an individual VIN is still awaiting repair.
2. **NHTSA 08V-160 / Saab E080101 / 15204:** [NHTSA acknowledgment](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2008/RCAK-08V160-6768.pdf) identifies **MY2002–08 9-5** in the administrative recall scope; the [specific owner letter](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2008/RCONL-08V160-4604.pdf) addresses **certain 2006–08 cars**. Preserve that difference. The intended remedy is revised impact-absorbing **A-pillar trim**, not an engine or headrest part. This is relevant to a MY2008 9-5.
3. **NHTSA 14V-318 / GM 14222:** [2014 manufacturer defect notice](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2014/RCDNN-14V318-3795P.pdf) covers **9-3 CONVERTIBLE MY2004–11** driver-side seat belt retractor cable and requires driver-retractor replacement. In this dataset it is scoped to the priority **2005–06 convertibles only**, **not sedans**, and separate passenger-retractor *special coverage* is not rewritten as a completed or current safety recall.

[NHTSA's VIN recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) generally **does not show safety recalls older than 15 years**, campaigns already repaired, manufacturer non-safety programmes, or some international vehicles. The [agency's recall FAQ](https://www.nhtsa.gov/resources-investigations-recalls) says safety recalls do not expire; that legal concept does **not** prove current repair parts availability or that an individual car has unfinished work. Preserve original campaign number, filed notice, exact year/body scope, real VIN, actual action, and proof of completion separately.
