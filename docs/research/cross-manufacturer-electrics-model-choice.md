# Manufacturer electrical replacement evidence: NG 9-3 B207 versus original 9-5 B235

Research date 2026-10-13. **Limited parts sourcing comparison, not a definitive vehicle reliability ranking.** Priority US/Canada NG9-3 MY2005–06 B207 FWD and original 9-5 MY2006–09 B235. Individual gearbox/body/installed part scope is still unknown.

| Component | NG 9-3 B207 candidate | Original 9-5 B235 candidate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alternator | [AS-PL A0047](https://as-pl.com/en/p/A0047), [applications](https://as-pl.com/en/tpu/A0047): 120 A / 5 grooves / PL_2200; supplier also lists Opel Vectra C/Signum Z20NET and Cadillac BLS | [AS-PL A0319](https://as-pl.com/en/p/A0319), [applications](https://as-pl.com/en/tpu/A0319/SAAB): 130 A / 6 grooves / PL_9105; current aftermarket candidate for original Saab 9-5 B235R |
| Starter | [AS-PL S1167S](https://as-pl.com/en/p/S1167S), [applications](https://as-pl.com/pl/tpu/S1167S): 12 V, 1.4 kW, 9 teeth, two bolt holes, Opel Z20NET and Saab B207 catalogue scope | **Not yet researched to equal depth**. Do not mistake a missing record for a missing replacement |
| Water pump / other prior evidence | Official GM/Saab common water-pump service SKU 12630084, shared aftermarket hub and pad leads | Saab-specific Nissens radiator and T7/PCV replacement histories; complete cross-brand mapping not yet studied |

An AS-PL part listed against multiple brands means the **same aftermarket SKU is sold for several vehicle applications**. That is not proof any original used Opel or Cadillac donor assembly will fit a real Saab. A0047 and A0319 are plainly distinct in amperage, belt grooves and plug; do not interchange them on generic 'Saab turbo' description. Opel Vectra and Cadillac BLS are not automatically available in a US salvage yard.

## Provisional significance for choosing the best rebuild base

The B207 9-3 currently has **more documented cross-brand sourcing leads** for selected components. This could improve service accessibility once part-specific US prices and actual VIN fit are confirmed. **It is not evidence of a lower failure rate.** The original 9-5 has Saab-specific alternatives and may be a better buy if the body, engine and transmission are sound. A failed high-cost gearbox, rusted structural mount or inaccessible ECU can overwhelm savings on starters and alternators.
