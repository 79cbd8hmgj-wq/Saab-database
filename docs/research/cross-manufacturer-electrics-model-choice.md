# Saab 9-3 B207 vs original 9-5 B235 — shared electrical parts, not yet a vehicle ranking

Research date **2026-10-13**. US/CA study scope: MY2005–06 NG9-3 B207 FWD and MY2006–09 original 9-5 B235. All listed parts are **supplier-catalogue leads**; there is no inspected vehicle VIN, tested fitment, dated U.S. landed quote or comparable durability study.

The 9-3 A0047 and 9-5 A0319 have **different pulley-groove count, output and plug**, so treating all Saab turbo alternators as interchangeable is demonstrably unsafe. Cataloguing one AS-PL alternator only for Saab does **not** establish that no other Opel/GM shared replacements exist for the B235 car. Conversely, a shared aftermarket AS-PL SKU **does not** establish a used Opel/GM original assembly is identical.

## Provisional decision observation (not a reliability score)

In our **small and unequally researched subset**, the **B207 9-3 has more identified multi-marque sourcing candidates**: hub, pump, alternator and starter. That may improve service sourcing options *if* a U.S. vendor has the exact confirmed SKU, but it is **not** a verified savings, shorter repair time or a quantified lifespan advantage. Opel and Vauxhall were not broadly sold in the US, so European catalogue cross-references do not by themselves confer local salvage-yard availability.

We must compare engines, gearboxes, ECU faults, correct parts programming, body corrosion and brakes. The T8 ECU repair and heat issues versus the 9-5's T7 ignition and B235 engine history are not settled by charging-system commonality. For a cheap non-running or rusty Northeast project Saab, serious structural repairs and expensive internal gearbox/engine faults carry a much larger uncertainty than whether a 120A alternator can be purchased from multiple brands.

## What the next evidence must establish

- Original 9-3 and 9-5 VIN-specific WebEPC starter/alternator OE identity, correct year/gearbox/chassis variants, connector/regulator specifications, and measured physical interfaces.
- Current comparable US supplier prices, shipping/core charges, warranty and fitted labor—not transient European price listings.
- Independent durability and repair-frequency data for Trionic 8/7, transmissions, lubrication, cooling, and electrical systems.
- Body-style corrosion and safety inspection of an actual candidate, plus seller history and repair cost. No model winner without the condition of the proposed donor car.
