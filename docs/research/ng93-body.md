# NG9-3 body and chassis supplement

8 factory sources, 25 facts, 6 gaps. Platform identifier is standardized as `9-3-9440`. The supplement adds US/Canada MY2006 body data and factory chassis/wheel architecture without assigning a universal weight or inferring an Aero chassis.

Early-manual weights are provisional; wagon mass fields are missing. Height body assignment and front-track unit consistency remain unresolved.

Factory WIS verifies wheel bolt count and thread, offset and centering method. Exact numeric PCD and hub-bore dimensions were not found in the factory pages, so they remain gaps. The early US wheel-size restriction also conflicts with a larger wheel listed in the WIS matrix; market/edition approval must resolve it.

Steering data is explicitly B207 hydraulic. Electro-hydraulic pump figures found elsewhere were excluded from B207 facts. Front and rear architecture and adjustment descriptions are recorded; Sport-versus-Standard differences do not imply trim fitment.
