# OG 9-5 structural and corrosion research

Research date: 2026-10-12.

## Scope

First factory-document pass: **12 sources, 16 facts, 1 narrowly documented repair plug and 5 research gaps** in `data/datasets/og95-structure.json`. Eleven sources are from MY2006 WIS body/repair sections and one is from MY2003 WIS corrosion theory. Scope is **not** a claim that these components deteriorate at a particular rate or that every year shares the same repair geometry.

## Body structure and documented repair constraints

- WIS says original body integrity depends on defined member geometry and a controlled repair method. Serious deformation calls for alignment-bench measurement; attachment points for the powertrain subframe must have correct relative location. [Checking body dimensions](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/checking-body-dimensions).
- Factory diagrams include replaceable subframe nut pieces accessed through the floor, with anti-corrosion treatment and a factory plug in the procedure. These are attachment and fastening **inspection zones**, not documented high-incidence corrosion failures. [Front-subframe rear nut](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/rear-nut-piece-front-subframe) and [rear-subframe nut](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/nut-piece-rear-subframe).
- Sill, rear wheelhouse and rear-floor repair methods specifically deal with reinforcements, spot/plug welding and restoration of protective coatings. Surface rust cannot be declared harmless if underlying reinforcement is unknown. [Rear sill](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/section-of-side-panel-rear-sill); [rear wheelhouse](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/rear-outer-wheel-housing); [sedan rear floor](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/section-of-rear-floor-4d).
- Partial rear frame-member replacement has a source-defined cut boundary; greater damage requires complete replacement. [Rear frame-member section](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/part-of-rear-frame-member).
- Some factory A-pillar joints use MIG brazing; regular welding is expressly prohibited as a substitute at those joints. [Lower A-pillar](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/adjustment-replacement/a-pillar-lower-part).

## Corrosion and lifting

Saab factory material attributes corrosion to the iron-water-oxygen reaction and acceleration by salts. Zinc, paint, seam sealing and cavity treatment provide protective barriers; they are not proof of condition on a 20-year-old car. [Corrosion](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2003/8-body/paint-and-surface-treatment/technical-description/corrosion). Factory lifting instructions specify front subframe/towing-eye, side jack brackets, and rear towing-eye/towbar points, while expressly warning against rear-axle and spare-wheel-well lifting. Follow the diagram and qualified safe-lift practice. [Lifting/attachment points](https://saabwisonline.com/9-5-9600/2006/8-body/body/technical-data/lifting-and-attachment-points).

## Use for project-car screening

For an uninspected rust-belt example, the *research-based inspection plan* is to request clear underside and inside-wheelhouse photographs showing mounting points, jacking areas, structural sills/reinforcements, rear frame members and lines; then inspect physically on a suitable lift with qualified structural assessment as needed. This prioritization is project inference, not a Saab official rust-frequency ranking.

A rusted rear subframe is a **major purchase-screening concern** because the rest of the body attachment geometry and adjacent load-bearing members may also require examination. A documented replacement procedure alone does not make an unsafe or deformed shell repair economical or suitable for novice DIY work.

## What remains unknown

No quantitative corrosion-rate or cross-model-year failure data was found in the sources reviewed. No factory body-thickness acceptance cutoff, current regional roadworthiness criterion, exact subframe assembly interchange or verified installed-car geometry was entered. Five explicit gap records preserve these needs. No safety clearance for any individual car is implied.
