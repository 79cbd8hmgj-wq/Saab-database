# OG Saab 9-5 (9600): initial specification research

This pass records 90 source-backed facts from 30 factory-manual pages, plus 7 gaps. Sources are Saab factory material reproduced by independent mirrors; the mirrors are not Saab. Retrieval date: 2026-10-12.

The JSON is the authoritative scoped extraction. It preserves each year, engine variant, market statement, test condition and gearbox code when documented. A missing market field means the source does not establish North American applicability. MY2005 dominates the generic WIS coverage; independently retrieved MY2004 and MY2009 pages support only their specified facts. No automatic model-year range expansion is used.

## Discrepancies that matter for replacement analysis

Power ratings must retain their source labels. The MY2009 generic WIS B235R row and North American owner manual use different numbers, speeds and EEC/SAE labels. Neither entry should overwrite the other or be used to convert hp into PS. The owner manual supports the North American model profile; the WIS entry remains a generic engine-table specification.

The MY2005 2.3t owner manual and generic B235E WIS specify different NGK plug types. Both readings are recorded. Fitment stays provisional until the actual vehicle and market-specific factory service instructions identify the correct application. The owner-manual text contains an implausible inch/mm spark-gap pairing, which was excluded.

Brake configuration, rotor-detail and owner-manual pages disagree on several diameter/thickness entries. Each reading is preserved in facts 062–070 and gap 003; those facts are unsuitable as automatic order selections. Actual rotor dimensions, caliper/carrier identity and configuration are the missing discriminators.

The wheel-alignment mirror includes malformed minute values and a conflicting 15-inch linear toe entry. Only unambiguous degree targets were extracted. Chassis type and ride-height checking still need the associated procedure before applying these targets.

## Reliability-first selection implications

The MY2004 WIS explicitly describes a new four-cylinder crankcase-ventilation design. This provides a documented production distinction, but does not quantify failure rates or establish the condition of a used engine. Oil-service history, physical PCV inspection, measured oil pressure and actual cooling/rust condition remain required for a purchase decision. The specifications support inspection criteria and part identification; they do not replace those observations.

Later North American owner-manual severe-use instructions provide an additional oil-service interval for their listed usage patterns. That documented instruction is kept year-specific. A present-day maintenance recommendation, modern approved-fluid substitutions, and a statistical model-year ranking were not inferred.
