**Goal:** Photograph, identify and SORT the Saabs. **Do not commit to buying a nonrunner because a seller names one cheap part.** U.S. originally sold 9-3/9-5, factory automatic, FWD, nonconvertible only.

## Every Saab: one quick pass

**S__** (assign sequential ID)  **MY:** ___  **9-3/9-5:** ___  **body:** ___  **automatic / FWD confirmed?** ___

**ASK:** When last running? When last moved? Why parked? What *tested* fault? Any key? Clear title and lawful transfer paperwork? Asking price? Can roll/steer for towing?

**PHOTOS:** Front • side • rear • odometer/dash • engine bay • visibly safe-to-see rust • **VIN privately**. Do not upload full VIN, title, plate or seller details to the public GitHub repository.

**LOOK:** Rear subframe AND mounts/body floor • rocker/lifting areas • front suspension anchors • wheel arches • brake/fuel lines • engine/wiring completeness • visible leaks. **Do not climb beneath or jack on a corroded car.**

**DECIDE** (temporary, not a buying verdict):
- **STOP:** excluded model (manual/convertible/XWD); obvious unsafe load-bearing corrosion, fuel/brake danger or confirmed major internal engine/transmission damage.
- **HOLD:** no VIN/title/key/working diagnosis; cannot safely view rust; "just a catalytic converter"; uncertain no-start; unknown engine/gearbox. Request evidence.
- **FINALIST:** correct scope and promising body from safe external view, keys/paperwork plausible, seller claim supported by test/invoice. Needs qualified inspection **before any purchase approval**.

### Especially the 2008 9-5 wagon

**Rear-subframe rust:** Is it ONLY the removable subframe, or is the **body anchor/captive-nut/rear floor/rail** rotten too? If not verified, HOLD/STOP for structural specialist. **Clogged catalytic converter:** What test proves a restriction, at which component, and why did it clog? P0420 or a seller guess is not proof.

### What not to do at the lot

No jumping/cranking/road testing a nonrunner without a safe mechanical clearance; no starting suspected oil-starved/hydrolocked/seized engines, no hot coolant caps, no open-flame fluid tests, no clearing codes and no driving with brake/fuel/structural hazards.
